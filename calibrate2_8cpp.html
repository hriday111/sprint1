Detailed Description

Two-point blob calibration + linear ROI interpolation check.

Moves the servo to pos0 (default 400) and pos1 (default 420), detects the three disc blobs at each end, and writes an interpolation model. Then steps through every position in between, evaluates the linear formula for blob centres, measures intensity, and writes a CSV so you can plot whether intensity stays flat (good tracking) before wiring this into sweep_run.

Usage: ./calibrate2 [exposure_ms=0.1] [pos0=400] [pos1=420] [n_blobs=3] [interp_path] [csv_path]

Does not stop servo_calib (FIFO closed without sending -1).
