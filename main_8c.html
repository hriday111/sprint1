Detailed Description

servo_calib entry point: home the prism, then serve FIFO commands.

Initializes file+console logging under logs/, enables core-dump handlers, creates world-writable FIFOs under /run/sprint1/, loads scan parameters from PRM_FILE (values 0–999), parks at prep, and blocks in serve_fifo until command -1.
