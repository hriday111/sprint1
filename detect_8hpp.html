Find dark filled marks and store one elliptical blob ROI per mark.

Each ROI is the fitted disc ellipse (slightly inset), used as the intensity mask by sweep_run. Names: ring1 top-left, ring2 top-right, then lower marks.

Parameters
grayMono8 crop (CAM_CROP_* geometry).
nwantDesired number of marks (clamped to 1..ROI_MAX).
outCleared and filled with detected blobs.
Returns
0 on success (including fewer marks than requested), -1 if gray is invalid.
